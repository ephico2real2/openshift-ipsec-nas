SELF-SERVICE

Application teams answer their own question

"Is my pod's NAS path working?" One row per pod: its node, and that node's tunnel UP, DOWN or NO COLLECTOR.

No ticket: the metrics hold no personal or health data, so readers need only view in the project and cluster-monitoring-view.

Self-service is a design goal, not an afterthought. The storage section lists every claim on the NAS and every pod that mounts one, with its node's tunnel state. An application team that thinks its NFS is stuck can look first. Access was decided for this: the metrics carry no personal or health data, so the platform grants cluster-monitoring-view to groups, and a reader of the project sees the dashboard.
