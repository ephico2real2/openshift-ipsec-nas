THE NORTH STAR

Every node: its own certificate, tunnel and report

Its own certificate

From the enterprise CA, through cert-manager; renewed by itself.

Its own tunnel

An NNCP for that node only, once its certificate is in place.

Its own report

The node's tunnel, certificate and NFS state, every 30 s, as Prometheus metrics.

The north star, once the NAS team is on board: every node that mounts the NAS has its own certificate, its own tunnel, and reports its own state, with no step by hand. One pod per node, from our in-house DaemonSet, does the certificate part and the reporting part.
