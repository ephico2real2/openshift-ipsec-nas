This is the end-to-end flow. People do day 0 once per cluster; from then on, scaling a MachineSet up is all it takes for a new worker to get its certificate from the enterprise CA and its tunnel to the NAS. If the NAS refuses a node, its tunnel is down and the IpsecNasTunnelDown alert fires for that node.
