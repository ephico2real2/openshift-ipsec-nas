How Option B gives every node its own certificate and tunnel

OpenShift nodes run RHCOS, configured by pools, not one by one. Option B gives each node its own certificate from the enterprise CA and its own tunnel to the NAS, with cert-manager, Kyverno and one DaemonSet, and no step by hand. Solid boxes are shipped and measured in the lab; dashed boxes and amber text are proposed or still to agree.
