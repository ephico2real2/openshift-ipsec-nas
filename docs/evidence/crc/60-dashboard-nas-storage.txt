# The dashboard section "Storage on the NAS (csi-driver-nfs)": its queries run against CRC's Thanos Querier (platform and
# user workload metrics, the route of thanos-querier in openshift-monitoring), then rendered in Grafana 13.2.3 and Perses
# v0.54.0 run locally on podman and reading the same Thanos. CRC 4.22.7, 2026-10-05 03:05-03:35Z.

## 1. What OpenShift's monitoring already has (kube-state-metrics, platform Prometheus)
  kube_persistentvolume_info{csi_driver="nfs.csi.k8s.io"}: 3 series, e.g. persistentvolume pvc-c98412b4-...,
    storageclass ipsec-nas-team-a, reclaim_policy Delete,
    csi_volume_handle 192.168.64.8#export-team-a#csi/ipsec-nas-team-a/app-data#pvc-c98412b4-...#
  kube_persistentvolumeclaim_info{storageclass=~"ipsec-nas.*"}: 3 series (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, storageclass, volumename)
  kube_storageclass_info{provisioner="nfs.csi.k8s.io"}: ipsec-nas-csi, ipsec-nas-team-a, ipsec-nas-team-b, all Delete, Immediate
  kube_pod_spec_volumes_persistentvolumeclaims_info{namespace=~"ipsec-nas.*"}: 3 series (namespace, pod, persistentvolumeclaim)
  kubelet_volume_stats_used_bytes{namespace=~"ipsec-nas.*"}: 1972371456 for all three claims: the NAS's file system
    (df on the NAS: 1.9G used), not each claim; left out of the dashboard.
  No collector series carries the NAS's IP (ipsec_nas_tunnel_info: connection, node, peer_id, pod): the NAS IP, export
  and directory are split out of the volume handle with label_replace.

## 2. The queries, as in charts/ipsec-nas/files/ipsec-nas.json ($node = .*), and their results
  [claims]
    (max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, storageclass, volumename) (kube_persistentvolumeclaim_info) * on (volumename) group_left (nas_ip, export, directory, reclaim_policy) max by (volumename, nas_ip, export, directory, reclaim_policy) (label_replace(label_replace(label_replace(label_replace(kube_persistentvolume_info{csi_driver="nfs.csi.k8s.io"}, "nas_ip", "$1", "csi_volume_handle", "([^#]*)#.*"), "export", "/$1", "csi_volume_handle", "[^#]*#([^#]*)#.*"), "directory", "$1", "csi_volume_handle", "[^#]*#[^#]*#([^#]*)#.*"), "volumename", "$1", "persistentvolume", "(.*)"))) * on (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim) group_left (phase) max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, phase) (kube_persistentvolumeclaim_status_phase == 1)
  [pods]
    (0 * (max by (namespace, pod, persistentvolumeclaim) (kube_pod_spec_volumes_persistentvolumeclaims_info) * on (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim) group_left (nas_ip, export) max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, nas_ip, export) (max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, storageclass, volumename) (kube_persistentvolumeclaim_info) * on (volumename) group_left (nas_ip, export, directory, reclaim_policy) max by (volumename, nas_ip, export, directory, reclaim_policy) (label_replace(label_replace(label_replace(label_replace(kube_persistentvolume_info{csi_driver="nfs.csi.k8s.io"}, "nas_ip", "$1", "csi_volume_handle", "([^#]*)#.*"), "export", "/$1", "csi_volume_handle", "[^#]*#([^#]*)#.*"), "directory", "$1", "csi_volume_handle", "[^#]*#[^#]*#([^#]*)#.*"), "volumename", "$1", "persistentvolume", "(.*)"))) * on (namespace, pod) group_left (node) max by (namespace, pod, node) (kube_pod_info{node=~"$node"})) + on (node) group_left () max by (node) (ipsec_nas_tunnel_up)) or (0 * (max by (namespace, pod, persistentvolumeclaim) (kube_pod_spec_volumes_persistentvolumeclaims_info) * on (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim) group_left (nas_ip, export) max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, nas_ip, export) (max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, storageclass, volumename) (kube_persistentvolumeclaim_info) * on (volumename) group_left (nas_ip, export, directory, reclaim_policy) max by (volumename, nas_ip, export, directory, reclaim_policy) (label_replace(label_replace(label_replace(label_replace(kube_persistentvolume_info{csi_driver="nfs.csi.k8s.io"}, "nas_ip", "$1", "csi_volume_handle", "([^#]*)#.*"), "export", "/$1", "csi_volume_handle", "[^#]*#([^#]*)#.*"), "directory", "$1", "csi_volume_handle", "[^#]*#[^#]*#([^#]*)#.*"), "volumename", "$1", "persistentvolume", "(.*)"))) * on (namespace, pod) group_left (node) max by (namespace, pod, node) (kube_pod_info{node=~"$node"})) - 1)
  [notbound]
    count(max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim) (kube_persistentvolumeclaim_status_phase{phase!="Bound"} == 1) and on (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim) (max by (namespace, persistentvolumeclaim, storageclass) (kube_persistentvolumeclaim_info) * on (storageclass) group_left () max by (storageclass) (kube_storageclass_info{provisioner="nfs.csi.k8s.io"}))) or vector(0)
  claims: 3 rows, Bound: ipsec-nas-csi-demo/app-data ipsec-nas-csi /export csi/ipsec-nas-csi-demo/app-data Delete;
          ipsec-nas-team-a/app-data ipsec-nas-team-a /export-team-a csi/ipsec-nas-team-a/app-data Delete; the same for team b
  pods: 3 rows, pod nas-demo-... on crc, value 1 (tunnel up); namespaces 3; NAS pods without a working tunnel 0
  Forced cases (the tunnel series swapped in the query):
    ipsec_nas_tunnel_up{node="none"} (no collector on the node): every row -1; the check counts 3
    0 * ipsec_nas_tunnel_up (tunnel down): every row 0; the check counts 3
  Claims not Bound: a class pointing at /export-does-not-exist and a claim of it (namespace ipsec-nas-pending-test):
    the claim Pending, ProvisioningFailed "failed to mount nfs server"; the check counted 1; the claims table does not
    list it (no volume yet). Class, claim and namespace removed afterwards.
