## 1. What the driver does on a resize (source, tag v4.13.4)
  pkg/nfs/controllerserver.go:470-483 ControllerExpandVolume checks the volume ID and the capacity range, logs, and
  returns CapacityBytes = the requested size. It sets no quota and does not ask for a node-side step. The driver
  advertises EXPAND_VOLUME (pkg/nfs/nfs.go:111). On NFS a claim's size is recorded, never enforced, before or after.

## 2. Which StorageClass fields can change on a live class
  Kubernetes ValidateStorageClassUpdate (pkg/apis/storage/validation/validation.go, release-1.35) forbids updates to
  parameters, provisioner, reclaimPolicy and volumeBindingMode only. allowVolumeExpansion and mountOptions are
  mutable; the live kubectl patch of section 0 succeeded.
