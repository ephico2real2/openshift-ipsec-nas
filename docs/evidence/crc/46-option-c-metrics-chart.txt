## 5. Finding: two shared alerts could not fire on OpenShift (Option B too), and the fix (15:28-16:15Z)
  User workload monitoring adds namespace="<the rule's namespace>" to every selector of a project's rules. Two rules of
  shared/collector/prometheus-rule-groups.yaml read platform series, which carry namespace=openshift-monitoring:
    IpsecNasExporterMissing, as loaded (/api/v1/rules):
      kube_node_role{namespace="kcs-ipsec",role="worker"} unless on (node) ipsec_nas_collect_timestamp_seconds{namespace="kcs-ipsec"}
    IpsecNasNfsWithoutTunnel, as loaded:
      sum by (node) (label_replace(rate(node_nfs_requests_total{namespace="kcs-ipsec"}[5m]), ...)) > 0 and on (node) ...
  Their platform selectors, queried on their own through Thanos Querier:
    kube_node_role{namespace="kcs-ipsec",role="worker"}             -> no series
    kube_node_role{role="worker"}                                   -> 1 series, namespace=openshift-monitoring job=kube-state-metrics node=crc
    count(node_nfs_requests_total{namespace="kcs-ipsec"})           -> no series
    count by (namespace, job) (node_nfs_requests_total)             -> namespace=openshift-monitoring job=node-exporter: 99
  So both expressions could never return a series. They fired on kind (evidence kind/02), where kube-prometheus-stack
  enforces no namespace.
