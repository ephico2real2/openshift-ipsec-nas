ipsec-nas is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

What happens next, by itself, for every node with the labels in nodeSelector:
  1. Kyverno creates a Certificate, and cert-manager issues it from ClusterIssuer "{{ .Values.clusterIssuer }}".
  2. The cert-sync pod on that node imports it and labels the node ipsec.kcs.io/cert-ready=true.
  3. Kyverno creates the NNCP, and NMState brings up the tunnel to {{ .Values.nas.ip }} ({{ .Values.ipsec.type }} mode).

Check it:
  oc get certificate -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  oc get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o wide
  oc get nncp,nnce | grep ipsec-nas
  oc debug node/<node> -q -- chroot /host ipsec trafficstatus
